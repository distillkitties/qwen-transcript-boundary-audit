- full Shannon entropy formalization;
- Gaussian rate-distortion formalization;
- large-scale Qwen fine-tuning;
- production model extraction;
- that every metadata field leaks in every deployment.

It does claim:
- Lean-checked finite post-processing accounting;
- real Qwen inference via MLX;
- a concrete omitted-route-metadata false-pass experiment;
- reproducible raw prompts and raw model outputs.
